# Proposed review structure

## 1. Introduction
- Industrial and colloidal relevance of precipitated silica.
- Why wet-gel evolution matters for final product properties.
- Existing review coverage and the precise gap (to be established by comparison).
- Scope, terminology, and central question.

## 2. Silicate solution chemistry and precipitation
- Silicate speciation and solution conditions.
- Primary-particle formation and growth.
- Process variables and limits of available in-situ measurements.

## 3. Aggregation and gelation
- Aggregation kinetics and gel-point development.
- Fractal/network descriptors and their interpretation.
- Distinguish primary particles, aggregates, and gel clusters.

## 4. Mechanical fragmentation of precipitated silica gels
- Energy input and fragmentation methods.
- Fragment size, structural integrity, and reaggregation.
- Evidence gaps and scale/measurement limitations.

## 5. Consolidation and structural reorganisation
- Time-dependent volume change and network restructuring.
- Interparticle forces and model descriptions.
- Role of pH, temperature, ionic conditions, and sample geometry where evidenced.
- Limits of applying soil-consolidation analogies or molecular models.

## 6. Syneresis: natural and enforced
- Definitions and experimental protocols.
- Natural syneresis kinetics.
- Mechanically enforced syneresis and correlations to natural behaviour.
- Influence of pH, temperature, and sample size.
- Conditions under which accelerated tests are transferable.

## 7. From wet-gel structure to final silica properties
- Porosity and pore-size distribution.
- Specific surface area and silanol/surface chemistry.
- Aggregate/agglomerate size and morphology.
- Permeability and mechanical stability.
- Wet-to-dry transformations: avoid conflating syneresis with drying shrinkage.

## 8. Integrated process–structure–property picture
- Synthesis across stages.
- Which mechanisms are directly supported and which remain hypotheses.
- Proposed conceptual framework, with confidence levels.

## 9. Open questions and research opportunities
- Missing in-situ and multiscale measurements.
- Comparability and reproducibility of protocols.
- Transfer from model systems to industrial precipitation.
- Priorities for future work that do not assume access to new experiments in this review project.

## 10. Conclusions
- Concise evidence-weighted answers to the central question.
- Practical and scientific implications, bounded by evidence.
